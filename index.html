Privacy Policy: How We Handle Your Data

A quote request often includes more than a name and an email address. Buyers share product details, container plans and, in many cases, drawings or plant layouts. This policy explains what Jvan Pack collects through its website, forms, WhatsApp, email and factory visits, why we use it, how long we keep it and how to ask us to correct or delete it.

Key takeaways

  • What we collect.

    Your name, email address, country and company, the product and container details you provide, any files you attach and basic website usage data.

  • Why we use it.

    To answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, build and ship your machine and support it after delivery.

  • No data sales.

    Jvan Pack does not sell, rent or lease personal information.

  • Defined retention.

    Each category of data has a stated keeping period. The full schedule is in the retention section.

  • Your cookie choice.

    Only cookies the site needs to function load before you choose. You can change your choice at any time.

    Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent. A request by email is enough to start.

Who is responsible for your data

Wenzhou Jvan Machinery Co., Ltd. (“Jvan Pack”, “we” and “us”) determines why and how your personal information is used, and is responsible for it. [Client to confirm: that this entity is responsible for all processing described in this policy.] Our address is Room 707, RenHui Building, No.995 Ouhai Avenue, Ouhai District, Wenzhou City 325000, Zhejiang Province, China.

This policy applies when you:

  • visit jvanpack.com
  • submit the quote form or the contact form
  • write to us by email or WhatsApp, or call us
  • request certificates, documents or a factory visit
  • meet our team at an exhibition or visit our factory
  • ask for support on a machine you own

It does not cover other websites we link to or our independent local partners, each of which has its own policy.

Personal information means any detail that identifies you or can be used to identify you, such as your name, email address or telephone number. A product formula or plant drawing may not name anyone, yet it remains commercially sensitive. We treat it with the same care. The security section explains how.

This website does not take card payments. If a visit or contract requires a document of that kind, we will request it in a separate message and explain why.

How we protect what you send

Access to your information is restricted to staff who need it to respond to you, and we rely on the security measures of our website, email and storage providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection. Where a data breach is likely to harm you, we will notify you and the relevant authority within the time and under the conditions the applicable law sets.

Drawings, formulas and layouts

A product formula, drawing or plant layout is business information as well as personal information. We use it to specify, quote, build, test and support your machine, and for no other purpose. If your company needs a confidentiality agreement before you share files, ask us by email or WhatsApp. We will reply before you upload anything.

Practical steps when sharing files

Keep faces and ID badges out of fault videos. Send contracts and formal documents by email, so both sides hold a written record.

Your rights and how to use them

Depending on the law that applies to you and on the circumstances of your request, you may ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your information
  • limit or stop the way we use it
  • object to a particular use
  • move your information to another company, where the law allows
  • stop using your email address for marketing
  • explain how we handle your information

If you gave us consent, you may withdraw it at any time. The required answers on a form are necessary for us to reply to you. Everything else is optional.

How to ask. Email jvan@jvanpack.com with the subject “Privacy request”, or use the privacy request form. Include the email address you used when you wrote to us, so we can find your records. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we share or delete anything, which protects you from unauthorised requests. We confirm receipt promptly and reply within 30 days, or sooner where the law that applies to you sets a shorter time. Ordinary requests are free of charge. Ordinary sales enquiries follow the separate reply time shown on the contact page.

If we cannot act on a request, for example because the law requires us to keep a record, we explain why. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the data protection authority in your country. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, that is your national data protection authority.

Children, links and changes

Children

Our website is intended for business buyers, not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us data, tell us. We will review it and delete it unless the law requires otherwise.

Links and embedded content

The website links to YouTube, Facebook and WhatsApp. These companies operate their own services, and their policies apply when you open a link or play a video. Please read them first.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. For a material change, such as a new type of data or a new purpose, we also display a notice on the quote form and the contact form before the change takes effect. Where the law requires your consent to the change, we ask for it again.
